# 0001: Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-09-24. Status: accepted.

## Context

Remit is meant to be built over years, by people who were not in the room for its early decisions.
A decision that is not written down with its reasons gets reversed by someone who cannot see why it was made.

## Decision

Every decision that constrains more than one module, or that a reasonable engineer might make differently, is recorded here as a numbered file: context, decision, consequences, and what would change it.
A superseded decision stays, marked superseded, with a link to the one that replaced it.

## Consequences

Decisions cost a page of writing.
In exchange, the reason for every constraint is one `grep` away.
